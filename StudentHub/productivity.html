Productivity

Make your time easier to manage with simple, realistic habits.

Plan your week

Write down classes, deadlines, and personal commitments. Add study blocks around your fixed activities.

Prioritize tasks

Start with urgent or important work. Break large assignments into smaller tasks that can be completed in one sitting.

Reduce distractions

Keep only the materials you need nearby. Silence unnecessary notifications during focused study sessions.

Use a task list

Write tasks with clear action words, such as “review chapter two” instead of “study.” Mark tasks complete when finished.

Take useful breaks

Stand up, stretch, drink water, or take a short walk between sessions to refresh your attention.

Review your progress

At the end of the week, note what worked, what took longer than expected, and what you want to adjust.

Example daily plan

  • Morning: Review class notes and set priorities.
  • Afternoon: Attend classes and complete one important task.
  • Evening: Practice a skill or revise a topic.
  • Before bed: Prepare a short plan for tomorrow.

This is only an example. Adjust it to your schedule, responsibilities, and energy levels.
