Career Guidance

Take practical steps toward internships, projects, and future opportunities.

Prepare your CV

Keep your CV easy to scan. Include education, relevant skills, projects, training, and contact details. Check spelling before sharing it.

Build projects

Small finished projects can demonstrate what you know. Explain the problem, your approach, the tools used, and what you learned.

Maintain a portfolio

Use a portfolio to collect selected projects, screenshots, short descriptions, and links to source code or live demos.

Practice interviews

Prepare to explain your work clearly. Practice common technical questions and describe how you approach unfamiliar problems.

Learn from feedback

Ask teachers, peers, or mentors to review your work. Use specific feedback to improve one area at a time.

Keep learning

Choose a skill relevant to your goals and create a manageable weekly practice plan. Track your progress instead of trying to learn everything at once.

Project idea: Complete one small project, write a clear README, and publish it on GitHub. This gives you something concrete to discuss in applications.
