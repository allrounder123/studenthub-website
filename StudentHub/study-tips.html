Study Tips

Build a study routine that works for your schedule and learning style.

01 · Plan

Set clear goals

Choose two or three realistic tasks for each study session. A clear target makes it easier to begin and measure progress.

02 · Focus

Study in short sessions

Work for a focused period, then take a short break. Adjust the session length to the difficulty of the topic and your concentration.

03 · Recall

Test yourself

Close your notes and explain the topic from memory. Practice questions can reveal what you understand and what needs review.

04 · Review

Revise regularly

Return to important material over several days instead of leaving all revision until the night before an exam.

05 · Notes

Write useful notes

Summarize key ideas in your own words. Use headings, examples, and diagrams when they make a concept easier to remember.

06 · Balance

Protect your rest

Sleep, hydration, movement, and breaks support attention. Make a realistic plan instead of relying on last-minute study marathons.

Try this today: Pick one topic, study it without distractions, then write down five things you remember.
